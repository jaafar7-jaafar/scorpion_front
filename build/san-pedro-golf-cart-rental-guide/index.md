Golf carts are one of the most common ways for visitors to get around Ambergris Caye. ## Choose a 4 or 6-seater for your group

Start with the number of people who will ride together. A 4-seater suits a couple, a small family, or a group of up to four. A 6-seater gives a larger family or group of friends room to travel in one cart. Ask about luggage space separately, particularly if you plan to use the cart on arrival or departure day.

## Driving a golf cart on Ambergris Caye

- Traffic in Belize drives on the right-hand side of the road.
- Drive slowly in San Pedro town, where carts share narrow streets with cars, taxis, bicycles, and pedestrians.
- Some roads outside town are unpaved and can be bumpy or muddy after rain. Allow extra time.
- Use your headlights after dark, and park in well-lit places.
- Do not leave valuables in the cart, and remove the key when you park.
- Ask our team about current road conditions and permitted routes before longer trips.

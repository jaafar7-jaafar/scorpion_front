# Rental Terms & Policies

Source: https://www.scorpiongolfcarts.com/rental-terms

> Golf cart rental terms for Scorpion in San Pedro, Belize: booking confirmation, starting rates, free delivery, 24-hour free cancellation, drivers, and payment.

The key terms for renting a golf cart from Scorpion Golf Cart Rental in San Pedro, Ambergris Caye, Belize. Your full rental agreement is confirmed with you before your rental starts.

## Booking requests

Submitting the booking form on this website, or messaging us on WhatsApp or by phone, is a request for a rental. A booking is confirmed only when our team confirms availability, your cart, your quote, and your delivery time with you by WhatsApp, phone, or email.

## Rates and quotes

Published rates are starting daily rates: from $35 per day for a 4-seater golf cart and from $65 per day for a 6-seater. Your quote depends on your dates and the cart you choose. Before you confirm, ask us to state the currency, taxes, fuel arrangements, and any deposit included in your total.

## Delivery and pickup

Delivery and pickup are free at hotels, resorts, and Airbnb rentals in San Pedro. Pickup at the San Pedro airstrip (SPR) can be arranged. Please give us accurate accommodation details and a phone number we can reach during your stay.

## Cancellation

You can cancel free of charge up to 24 hours before your rental begins. Contact our team by WhatsApp or phone to cancel or change your booking.

## Drivers

Drivers must hold a valid driver's license. Confirm driver eligibility and any additional documents with our team before your rental starts. Drive carefully, follow local traffic rules, and ask us about permitted routes before longer trips.

## Payment

We accept cash and major credit and debit cards.

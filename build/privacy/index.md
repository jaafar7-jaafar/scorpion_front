# Privacy Policy

Source: https://www.scorpiongolfcarts.com/privacy

> How Scorpion Golf Cart Rental in San Pedro, Belize collects and uses information from booking requests, reviews, analytics, and WhatsApp or phone contact.

How Scorpion Golf Cart Rental collects and uses information when you visit this website, request a golf cart rental, or leave a review.

## Information you give us

- **Booking requests:** your name, email address, phone number, accommodation or delivery address, pickup and return dates and times, cart seating, decoration request, and any notes you add.
- **Reviews:** the name, rating, and comment you submit. Reviews are published on this website.
- **Messages and calls:** information you share when you contact us by WhatsApp or phone.

## How we use it

We use booking information to check availability, confirm your rental, arrange delivery and pickup, and contact you about your booking. We use reviews to show visitors what past guests say. We do not sell your personal information.

## Analytics and third-party services

This website may use Google Analytics to count page views and clicks on booking, phone, and WhatsApp buttons, which helps us understand how visitors find us. The service-area map is provided by Google Maps. WhatsApp conversations are handled by WhatsApp. These services process data under their own privacy policies.

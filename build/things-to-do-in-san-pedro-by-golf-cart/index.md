> Where to go on Ambergris Caye by golf cart: San Pedro town, Boca del Rio, Secret Beach, the north and south of the island, tour docks, and practical driving tips.

A golf cart turns Ambergris Caye into an easy road trip. Here are the places visitors most often drive to from San Pedro, Belize, plus practical tips for planning your day with a rental cart.

## Where to go on Ambergris Caye by golf cart

Stop 1

### San Pedro town

The heart of Ambergris Caye, with restaurants, shops, bars, grocery stores, and the beachfront. Streets in town are narrow and busy, so park your cart and explore the waterfront on foot.

Stop 2

### Boca del Rio

A neighborhood at the north end of town with a public beach area that is popular with locals and visitors for swimming and sunsets. It is an easy, short drive from most San Pedro accommodations.

Stop 3

### North Ambergris Caye

Cross the bridge at the north end of town to reach the northern part of the island, with resorts, beach bars, and restaurants spread along the road. Ask whether a bridge pass is included in your rental before you go.

Stop 4

### Secret Beach

One of the most popular golf cart trips on the island, on the calm west (lagoon) side of Ambergris Caye, north of town, with beach clubs and shallow turquoise water. The road can be rough, so allow plenty of time and check current conditions with our team.

Stop 5

### South Ambergris Caye

South of town the island is quieter, with residential areas, resorts, and lagoon views. It is a relaxed drive for an afternoon or a meal away from the busiest streets.

Stop 6

### Reef and snorkel tour docks

Snorkeling and diving trips to the Belize Barrier Reef, including Hol Chan Marine Reserve and Shark Ray Alley, leave by boat from docks in San Pedro. Drive your cart to your tour operator, then pick it up when you return.

## Exploring Ambergris Caye by golf cart: FAQ

### Can you drive a golf cart to Secret Beach on Ambergris Caye?

Many visitors drive a golf cart to Secret Beach on the west side of Ambergris Caye, north of San Pedro town. The road can be rough and conditions change with the weather, so allow plenty of time and ask our team about current conditions before you go.

### Can I drive a golf cart to snorkeling tours like Hol Chan Marine Reserve?

Hol Chan Marine Reserve and other reef trips are reached by boat. A golf cart is a convenient way to get from your accommodation to your tour operator's dock in San Pedro and back.

### Is it safe to drive a golf cart at night in San Pedro?

Drive slowly, use your headlights, and watch for pedestrians, bicycles, and unlit areas. Park in well-lit places and do not leave valuables in the cart.
